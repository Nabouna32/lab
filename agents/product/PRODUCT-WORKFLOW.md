# Product and documentation workflow

Use `agents/AGENT-CONTRACT.md` for collaboration, decision rights, plan validation and delivery; use `agents/PRODUCT-ISSUE-CONTRACT.md` for the mission plan and checkpoint.

Before changing product intent, read the relevant canonical specifications and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Challenge existing decisions when evidence or a better solution warrants it. Distinguish current product intent from implemented behavior and proposals.

For consequential changes to vision, product behavior, architecture, privacy or business direction, explain the evidence, consequences, materially different options and recommendation. Continue discussion through objections until the user explicitly identifies the retained decision. Never change direction silently.

After validation, update the canonical source and all dependent active documents consistently. Record a durable decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md` when the change is structurally significant. Put operational work plans in the active mission Issue, not in competing repository plan files. Keep deferred ideas in their canonical discovery record and link actionable work to Issues.
